HOW TO PLAY

Hit GENERATE to roll a random number. Every pattern it matches (contains a digit, is prime, is a palindrome, and dozens more) earns you a badge and some DP. Roll as many times as you want — there's no daily limit, so see how lucky you get.

DP stands for Digit Points — the currency you earn from badges, and what your overall rarity is based on.

OVERALL RARITY

Your overall rarity isn't just your single best badge — it's based on the total DP every badge you earned adds up to.
